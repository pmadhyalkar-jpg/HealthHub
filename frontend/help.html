Help & Platform Guidance

Step-by-step assistance for patients and medical practitioners.

Medical Emergency Notice: If you or someone around you requires emergency medical treatment, please do not use this web platform. Call immediate local emergency services:
National Emergency: 112 • Ambulance: 102 • Women Helpline: 1091

Patient User Guide

  • Create an Account: Register with basic information.
  • Search Doctors: Filter by specialization, city, and consultation fee.
  • Select a Slot: Real-time available slots calculated from doctor schedule.
  • Manage Bookings: View upcoming visits and cancel if needed from your dashboard.

Doctor Verification Guide

  • Register Profile: Provide qualifications, specialty, and clinic details.
  • Upload Certificate: Attach medical degree/registration (PDF/JPG/PNG ≤ 5MB).
  • Admin Audit: Platform administrator reviews the document in secure portal.
  • Go Live: Once verified, your weekly schedule unlocks for patient bookings.
